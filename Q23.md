# <span style="color:red">Individuals can do nothing to improve the environment; only governments and large companies can make a difference. To what extent do you agree or disagree?</span>


It is often argued that every person can't do to foster the environment more than governments and big corporations do. In my opinion, I partially agree because individuals are responsible for improving the environment with authorities and companies together. 
<br>
On the one hand, it is clear that each citizen should take a share of responsibility to protect the environment. Nowadays, household waste from families often accounts for a significant proportion of total waste discharged into the environment. Studies show that each person releases approximately two kilograms of waste, half of that is non-recylable products on a daily basis. If this small number is multiplied with the total number of people in the world, we should see an enormous amount of waste disposed by human, showing the adverse effects on the atmosphere and sea. Therefore, the average citizens should make noticeable impacts on the environment right now by adopting sustainable lifestyle which can be done by numerous modest acts every day.
<br>
On the other hand, I am of the opinion that governments and huge enterprises can do much more than individuals to improve the Earth. The authorities often plays a central role in protecting our environment because they govern their whole countries. By introducing strict laws and heavy punishment, they can deter people from polluting their living conditions. Large corporations, besides, can help reduce industrial waste discharged into the environment with environmentally friendly products and an effective waste management system. 
<br>
In conclusion, while I agree that individuals can do nothing to improve the environment, I also believe that the governments and large companies can do much more to do it too.
