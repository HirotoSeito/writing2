# <span style="color:red">It is a natural process for animal species to become extinct (e.g. Dinosaur, dodos,...). There is no reason why people should try to prevent this from happening. Do you agree or disagree?</span>
It is argued that people no need to prevent animal species from becoming extinct because it is  a natural process. Personally, I completely disagree with this statement as it is not because of the environmental conditions and is beneficial to human in a specific field.
<br>
The first and foremost reason why I do not agree with this view is the acceleration in devastating activities done by human. Before the existence of the human society, the extinction of species occurred generally due to environmental factors such as extreme weather. However, in the modern day, people speed up the extinction of various species, shown via the expansion of cities and removal of agriculture lands resulting in the loss of animal habitats. For example, bees which have great impacts on the development of fruits and vegetable are facing extinction because of the deforestation to serve growing human's demands. If they went extinct, there would be a food crisis in many countries.
<br>
Another reason that I disagree with this opinion is the lack of resources for scientific and pharmaceutical research. Most medical innovations rely on chemical compounds found in wild animals, which are used for particular diseases and ailments. For instance, substances from snakes can help treat patients with cancer or vascular diseases. Therefore, it is ideal for us to protect wildlife species because without them, it is challenging to find potential cures for future illnesses.
<br>
To conclude, I totally disagree with the opinion that letting animals become extinct is a natural selection since extinction is partly driven by human activities, and people take advantage of them for medical research, thus taking action to protect them from extinction is crucial.
