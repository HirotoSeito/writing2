# <span style="color:red">In the modern world, it is no longer neccessary to use food from animals or to product from animals such as medicine and clothing. Do you agree or disagree?</span>

Many people argue that eating animal-based diet and using products such as medicine and clothes derived from animals is unessential anymore nowadays. Personally, I totally agree this statement due to technological advancement in present day.
The first and foremost reason that I support this idea is the emergence of a more healthy diet and the shift towards artificial clothing. Firstly, instead of having meat or fish in every meals, people can follow a plant-based diet which provides sufficient nutrients for body to function normally and is especially a great eating habit to prevent various health problems such as obesity and tapeworm infection. Secondly, these days, we can replace animal's fur with artificial fur and fabric. For centuries, preceding farmers raised sheeps for wool used to make warm clothes as it kept human to avoid getting cold during winter. However, with the development of clothes made of artificial materials ranging from plastics to vegetable, it is unnecessary to exploit animals for clothing manufacturing.
Another reason that I support the view is the evolution of animal experiments. Testing on animals have been playing a central role of research on solution to incurable diseases although it often receives a large number of arguments for ethical concerns. Nevertheless, we now can alternate the testing with complicated but efficient computer programs providing more accurate results. For instance, computer-based testing has been used to test new drugs or cancer treatments, which is widely proven to be efficient by researchers. Therefore, these advancements in technology in these fields have made the use of animals no longer crucial in this modern world.
In conclusion, I am convinced that eating and using animal products such as medicine and clothing is not essential in present days owing to the emergence of advanced technology.
